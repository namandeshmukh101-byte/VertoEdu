VertoEdu Design System v1.0
Where AI Meets Education
Design Philosophy
Clean, modern SaaS UI focused on trust, simplicity, readability and AI-powered education.
Brand Identity
Use the VertoEdu logo featuring the dominant 'V', graduation cap and subtle AI flow. Place the finalized logo on the cover page after insertion.
Color Palette
Ice Blue #4F8EF7, Hover #3B82F6, Navy #1E3A5F, Background #F7FBFF, White #FFFFFF, Text #243B53, Secondary #6B7C93, Success #4CAF50, Warning #F4B400, Error #E57373.
Typography
Inter as primary font. Clear hierarchy and generous spacing.
Spacing & Layout
8px spacing system. 12-column desktop, 8-column tablet, 4-column mobile.
Components
Use shadcn/ui components consistently for buttons, cards, forms, dialogs, tables, tabs, alerts, badges, skeletons and toasts.
Dashboard
White cards, navy sidebar, ice blue highlights. Admin: KPIs. Teacher: tasks. Parent: student progress.
Forms
Inline validation, success/error toasts, friendly messages.
Responsive
Desktop, tablet and mobile support with no horizontal scrolling.
Accessibility
Keyboard navigation, contrast, focus states and readable text.
UX Rules
Always provide loading, empty and error states. Confirm destructive actions.
AI Coding Rules
Always follow the PRD, Brand Guidelines and Design System. Reuse components and preserve architecture.
Logo Note
Insert the finalized high-resolution VertoEdu logo (V + graduation cap + AI flow, tagline 'Where AI Meets Education') on the cover page and brand identity section.
